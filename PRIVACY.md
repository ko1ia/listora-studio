# Privacy Policy — Listora Studio

**Last updated:** November 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Listora Studio ("the Extension") is a browser extension designed to help Second Life Marketplace merchants manage product listings, edit product information, upload images, use reusable templates, and publish products more efficiently.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, processed, stored, and transmitted when using Listora Studio.

Listora Studio is an independent third-party product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially supported by Linden Lab.

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1. Second Life Marketplace Information

To provide its functionality, Listora Studio may access information available through the user's authenticated Second Life Marketplace session, including:

- Product names and descriptions
- Product prices and categories
- Product images and related media
- Keywords and product features
- Listing identifiers and publication status
- Product permissions and other listing settings
- Merchant identifiers required for listing operations

This information is used to display, edit, organize, and publish Marketplace listings at the user's request.

### 2.2. Locally Stored Information

Listora Studio uses browser extension storage to maintain information such as:

- Extension preferences and settings
- Reusable project templates
- Custom variables and product-specific values
- Local publishing history
- License activation status and installation tokens

This information helps users retain their settings and continue working across browser sessions.

### 2.3. License Verification Information

Listora Studio uses a license verification service hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

During license activation and verification, the Extension may transmit:

- License activation keys
- License or installation tokens
- Randomly generated installation identifiers
- Information necessary to determine license validity and expiration

This information is processed for license activation, verification, and prevention of unauthorized license use.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed only for purposes related to operating Listora Studio, including:

- Providing product editing features
- Managing Marketplace listings and product images
- Applying templates and dynamic variables
- Saving user preferences
- Performing user-requested publishing operations
- Maintaining local publishing history
- Activating and validating software licenses
- Diagnosing operational errors

Listora Studio does not use Marketplace product information for advertising or behavioral profiling.

## 4. Data Storage

### Local Browser Storage

Extension preferences, templates, custom variables, and publishing history are stored locally in the user's browser.

Local information may be deleted when the user clears extension data, removes the Extension, or deletes the corresponding browser profile.

### License Server

License-related information is processed through Cloudflare Workers and may be stored in Cloudflare D1 databases.

License records are maintained to administer active, expired, or revoked licenses and their associated installations.

## 5. Data Transmission and Third-Party Services

### Second Life Marketplace

Listora Studio communicates with `marketplace.secondlife.com` to perform listing management operations requested by the user.

These operations use the user's existing authenticated browser session.

Marketplace data submitted through the Extension is handled by Second Life Marketplace according to its applicable policies.

### Cloudflare

Cloudflare infrastructure is used to provide license activation and verification services.

Cloudflare may process technical information associated with requests to the license service as part of its infrastructure, security, and operational functions.

### Browser Translation Services

Where supported, Listora Studio may use translation capabilities provided by the user's browser.

The availability and data processing behavior of those features depend on the browser and its provider.

Users should consult their browser provider's privacy documentation for information about translation-related processing.

## 6. Data Sharing

Listora Studio does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information is transmitted to the services necessary to provide its described functionality, including Second Life Marketplace and the license verification infrastructure.

Listora Studio does not use collected information for targeted advertising.

## 7. Data Security

Listora Studio uses the browser's existing authenticated Marketplace session for product management operations.

Administrative license management credentials are not intended to be distributed within the Extension.

License verification requests are transmitted over HTTPS.

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect license information and reduce unauthorized access.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Data Retention

Locally stored information remains in browser extension storage until it is deleted or the relevant browser data is removed.

Server-side license information is retained as necessary to provide and administer licensing services.

Certain technical records may also be retained by infrastructure providers according to their applicable policies and configurations.

## 9. User Choices and Data Deletion

Users can stop Listora Studio from accessing Marketplace pages by disabling or uninstalling the Extension.

Users may remove locally stored information by clearing the Extension's storage or uninstalling it.

Deleting local browser data does not necessarily delete server-side license records.

To request assistance with license-related information or data deletion, users may contact the developer using the contact details below.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Listora Studio is intended for users who are eligible to operate Second Life Marketplace merchant accounts.

The Extension is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Extension, its features, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be available through the official Listora Studio documentation.

The date at the top of this document indicates when the policy was last updated.
